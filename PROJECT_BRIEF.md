# Cycling Club Ride Scheduling App

## What I'm building

A SaaS web app for cycling clubs to manage and schedule rides. The core problem it solves is that clubs currently post rides on Facebook, where the algorithm buries posts in the social feed and members miss them. The app provides a dedicated, reliable home for ride scheduling that isn't competing with the noise of a general social network.

## Longer term vision

Expand beyond ride scheduling into broader club management — events, volunteer callouts, member communication — similar in spirit to Spond but focused on cycling clubs.

## Tech stack

Next.js, Supabase (auth and database), multi-tenant architecture.

## Where I am

App is running locally. Early stage but functional. Have had an initial validation conversation with a cycling club committee member who confirmed the Facebook problem is real.

## About me

Self-taught JavaScript and React developer with 3-4 years of industry experience. Background in journalism. Active cyclist and runner. Building this as a side project with a 1-2 year runway, keeping it free initially to drive adoption.

## Current priorities

Getting the project to an MVP stage where:

- Admins can configure club settings
- Admins and riders can set up rides
- Admins and members can confirm attendance
